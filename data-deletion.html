Delete My Data

How to remove your data from Bottle Flip Master

Bottle Flip Master has no accounts and does not store your personal profile on our servers. This page explains where your data is and how to delete it.

1. Delete data stored in the App (your progress)

  1. In the App: Settings → Reset progress (this erases levels, stars, balances, collection and settings stored on your device), or
  2. Android: Settings → Apps → Bottle Flip Master → Storage → Clear storage, or
  3. Uninstall the App.

Resetting does not cancel subscriptions — see Subscriptions & Refunds.

2. Advertising data (Google)

  • Reset or delete your Advertising ID: Android Settings → Privacy → Ads → Delete advertising ID.
  • Open the consent options inside the App: Settings → Ad privacy options.
  • Manage or delete Google ad data: myadcenter.google.com and myactivity.google.com.

3. Purchase records (Google Play)

Order history is kept by Google. Manage it at pay.google.com. We keep only the transaction records needed for accounting and refunds as required by law.

4. Ask us to delete data we hold

We hold almost nothing about you. If you emailed us, we keep your email and message only to reply. To have that deleted, email gautamshubham@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my data” (include your Google Play order number if it relates to a purchase). We confirm within 7 days and complete deletion within 30 days, except records we must keep by law (such as transaction records).
