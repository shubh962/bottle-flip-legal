This Privacy Policy explains how TaskGuru Apps (“we”, “us”, “our”) handles information when you use Bottle Flip Master (the “App”). We designed the App to collect as little personal data as possible: there are no user accounts, and you do not have to give us your name, email or phone number to play.

2. Information we handle

2.1 Stored on your device

Your progress (levels, stars, objects discovered), currencies (coins, gems, caps, hearts), settings (sound, vibration, sensitivity, graphics) and flags for purchases are stored locally on your phone. We do not receive this data. Uninstalling the App or clearing its storage deletes it (see Delete My Data).

2.2 Purchases

Payments are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your card or payment details. We receive confirmation data such as the product ID, order ID and purchase token so we can deliver the item, restore it on request and (if enabled) verify it with Google.

2.3 Advertising and device data

The App uses Google AdMob. Google and its partners may collect and process device identifiers (including the Android Advertising ID), IP address, approximate region, device model, operating-system version, app version, language and ad interaction data, in accordance with the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

2.4 Diagnostics and analytics

The App records simple gameplay events (for example, level started or completed) only inside the App on your device to support game progression and functionality; they are not sent to our servers.

2.5 Contact

If you email us, we receive your email address and message and use them only to reply.

3. How we use information

  • Provide the game, deliver and restore purchases, prevent fraud and abuse.
  • Show advertising and measure its performance (Google AdMob).
  • Maintain local game state, settings, and gameplay balance on your device.
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms.

Legal bases (EEA/UK): performance of a contract (delivering the game and purchases), legitimate interests (security, fraud prevention), consent (personalised advertising and use of the Advertising ID where required) and legal obligation.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows banner, interstitial (full-screen) and optional rewarded video ads. In the EEA, UK and some other regions we ask for your consent with Google’s consent message (UMP) before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice any time in the App: Settings → Ad privacy options. You can also reset or delete your Advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads, and manage Google ad personalisation at myadcenter.google.com. Buying Remove Ads or VIP removes banner and full-screen ads; rewarded ads remain optional.

5. In-app purchases

Purchases (coin packs, heart packs, Remove Ads, Starter Pack, Legend bottles and VIP subscriptions) are processed by Google Play under Google’s terms. See Subscriptions & Refunds.

6. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with: (a) Google (Google Play for payments, AdMob for advertising); (b) service providers that help us operate the App under confidentiality obligations; (c) authorities when legally required; and (d) a successor if we are involved in a merger or sale of the business.

7. Children

The App is intended for a general audience of age 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us at GautamShubham962@gmail.com and we will delete it. For users below the age of digital consent in their country, use of the App requires parental permission. Parents can disable personalised ads through Google Family Link and Android ad settings.

8. Retention

Local game data stays on your device until you delete it. Purchase records are kept by Google under its own policies and by us only as long as needed for support, accounting and legal requirements. Because we do not operate user-data servers for this App, we do not retain server-side gameplay logs.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures (HTTPS connections, no storage of payment data, limited access). No method of electronic storage is perfectly secure.

10. International transfers

Google and other providers may process data in countries outside your own. Where required, transfers rely on lawful mechanisms such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

11. Your rights & choices

Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, UK, California and other US states, Brazil, India), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or port your data, to object to processing, to withdraw consent and to lodge a complaint with your data-protection authority. Because we hold no account data, most of your data is on your device (you control it) or with Google. To make a request about data we hold, email GautamShubham962@gmail.com; we respond within 30 days (or the period required by law) after verifying your request.
