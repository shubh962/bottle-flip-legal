The exact price, billing period and any introductory offer or free trial are shown in Google Play before you confirm. Prices are in your local currency and may include tax.

2. How subscriptions work

  • Payment is charged to your Google Play account when you confirm the purchase.
  • The subscription renews automatically for the same period at the then-current price unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period (Google Play may apply different timing; its rules prevail).
  • If a free trial or discounted introductory offer is offered, the unused part is forfeited when you buy a paid subscription where Google Play allows it, and billing starts when the trial ends unless you cancel before.
  • If we change the price, Google Play will notify you and, where required, ask for your consent before the new price applies.
  • When a subscription ends, VIP benefits stop. Items you already received (coins, hearts, skins you unlocked with coins) stay in your account.

3. Manage or cancel

Open Google Play Store → your profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Bottle Flip Master → Cancel subscription, or visit play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions. Uninstalling the App does not cancel a subscription.

4. Refunds

Purchases are processed and refunded by Google Play. To request a refund, go to your Google Play order history and choose Report a problem / Request a refund, or contact Google Play support. Google decides refund requests under its refund policy and applicable consumer law.

Because digital items are delivered immediately, consumable items (coins, hearts) are generally non-refundable once delivered, except where required by law (for example a defective item or unauthorised purchase) or Google’s policy. This does not affect your statutory rights. If you refund a purchase, we may remove the related items from your balance.

5. Restore purchases

One-time purchases and active subscriptions can be restored on a new device or after reinstalling: Settings → Restore purchases (use the same Google account). Consumed items (coins, hearts) cannot be restored.

6. Problems with a purchase

If you were charged but did not receive an item: restart the App with an internet connection and tap Restore purchases; if it does not appear, email gautamshubham@gmail.com with your Google Play order number (starts with GPA.) and we will help.

Parents: you can require authentication for every purchase in Google Play → Settings → Authentication, and set up Family Link purchase approvals.
